# Machine Learning & Data Science Consulting

### Transform your data into actionable insights

With my extensive experience as an AI Engineer at Apple, ML engineer at Meta, Applied Scientist at Amazon, and Data Scientist at JP Morgan Chase, I help organizations unlock the full potential of their data through machine learning and advanced analytics.

### What I Offer

**Machine Learning Strategy & Implementation**
- End-to-end ML pipeline design and implementation
- Model selection, training, and optimization
- A/B testing and experimentation frameworks
- Production ML system architecture

**Data Science Consulting**
- Exploratory data analysis and insights generation
- Predictive modeling and forecasting
- Statistical analysis and hypothesis testing
- Data visualization and storytelling

**Technical Leadership**
- ML team building and mentorship
- Code reviews and best practices
- Technology stack selection
- Process optimization and workflow design

### My Background

- **Current**: AI Engineer at Apple (Seattle)
- **Previous**: ML Engineer at Meta, Applied Scientist at Amazon, Data Scientist at JP Morgan Chase
- **Academic**: PhD in Physics (Cosmology) from University of Helsinki
- **Expertise**: Production ML systems, A/B testing, data pipelines, team leadership

### Industries I've Worked With

- **Finance**: Risk modeling, fraud detection, algorithmic trading
- **Technology**: Recommender systems, Search, user behavior analysis, growth metrics
- **E-commerce**: Demand forecasting, personalization, conversion optimization, product classification
